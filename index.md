The 636th Mt. Tabor OSG Group is a traditional outdoor organization that is open to adults and youth of all genders, and is dedicated to being an open and inclusive outdoor program.

The 636th Mt. Tabor is a registered member of the Outdoor Service Guides (OSG), which is committed to providing a traditional, community-oriented outdoor experience. **OSG welcomes all members regardless of race, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion (or no-religion) or other differentiating factors.** Our mission is to provide a positive learning environment within the context of democratic participation and social justice. We foster the development of members of all ages in an environment of mutual respect and cooperation.

At its heart, our program is an outdoor movement and that is a key part of its character. The appeal of true outdoor adventure appeals to the vagabond, pioneer and creative explorer within us all, and which is most evident in our youth. OSG activities focus on these elements, and encourage our members, parents, and volunteers to plan programs that take advantage of being outdoors &ndash; whenever and wherever possible!

Yours in traditional outdoor adventure,<br/>
